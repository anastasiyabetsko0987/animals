# Bird

![Bird](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3d/Grey_crowned_crane_%28Balearica_regulorum_gibbericeps%29.jpg)

Birds are a group of warm-blooded vertebrates constituting the class Aves, characterised by feathers, toothless beaked jaws, the laying of hard-shelled eggs, a high metabolic rate, a four-chambered heart, and a strong yet lightweight skeleton. Birds live worldwide and range in size from the 5.5 cm bee hummingbird to the 2.8 m common ostrich. There are over 11,000 living species and they are split into 44 orders.
